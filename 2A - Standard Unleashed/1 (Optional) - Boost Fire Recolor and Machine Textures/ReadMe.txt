Select ONLY one of the folders, and copy and paste the entire contents to the 'root' folder

In previous releases of my GX Unleashed mod, I only provide the option to recolor the booster fire with my changes to the fire size and color. The only alternative was to not copy over the files.

It is normally a lengthy, cumbersome process to combine my fire recolors with another modder's machine skin/texture modifications, so I decided to provide many of the various combinations of my fire recolor, superSANIC's fire recolor and machine texture mods.

The Unleashed boost fire recolors (done by me) generally use brighter colors, change the size of the fire based on the strength of their booster or top speed. 

The superSANIC's GXtreme boost fire recolors for certain machines uses very large fire (Fat Shark, Silver Rat), but otherwise he utilizes different colors and sizes compared to the Unleashed one.

The folder name describes which boost fire and machine text mod(s) it contains. I also include options that utilize the base game boost fire colors. Only GXtreme has alternate machine textures.
